## Slide 1 — Title
**Activating LeadWallet at school**  
Audience: teachers / form tutors / MLQ coordinators · 5–8 minutes

**Say:** Teachers guide. Parents approve once. Teachers do not switch wallets on.

---

## Slide 2 — What it is
LeadWallet = student’s **real ₦ rewards wallet**

| Not this | This |
|----------|------|
| Coins / XP | LeadWallet cash |
| Teacher switch | Parent one-time approve |

---

## Slide 3 — Roles
- **Student** — requests  
- **Parent** — Approves in Parent Portal  
- **Teacher** — coaches correct email  
- **MLQ admin** — credits rewards later  

---

## Slide 4 — Step 1 (screenshot 01)
1. Open MLQ → LeadWallet → Activate  
2. Enter **parent** email  
3. Tap **Request parent approval**  

**Stress:** email must match parent’s MLQ login.

---

## Slide 5 — Step 2 (screenshot 02)
Screen: **Waiting for parent approval**  
Student can tap **Refresh status** later.  
Parent can create account later with same email (within 7 days).

---

## Slide 6 — Step 3 (screenshot 03)
Parent: **Profile → Parent Portal → LeadWallet approvals → Approve**  
*(Email approval link is hidden / not for teacher briefing.)*

---

## Slide 7 — Step 4 (screenshot 04)
Student: Refresh → **LeadWallet is active** → **Open Wallet**  
Bank setup is **not** required for activation.

---

## Slide 8 — After activation
Earn (admin credits) → Withdraw (student bank) → Payout (admin)  
Activation ≠ cash already in hand.

---

## Slide 9 — Common issues
Wrong email · no parent account yet · stuck waiting · bank/BVN myth · “enable my whole class”

---

## Slide 10 — Script
> LeadWallet is where students keep real naira rewards. Student requests → parent approves once in Parent Portal → wallet activates. Teachers help with the correct parent email. Coins are separate; money appears after approved rewards.
